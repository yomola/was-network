December 2026 – Case Study: Chloe and Joanna

Faithful Witness, Influence & Sacrificial Support

1 Corinthians 1:11 Luke 8:1–3 Luke 24:10

Chloe and Joanna demonstrate the power of faithful witness and active support in God's work. Their lives show how personal integrity, sacrificial giving, and partnership in ministry advance God's Kingdom and strengthen the church community.

Week 1 – Chloe's Care for the Church

Week 1

Chloe's Care for the Church

Key Scriptures: 1 Corinthians 1:11; Acts 6:1–7

Weekly Focus: Identifying problems in the church and taking action through prayerful, faithful influence.

December 1 – Observing the Needs

Scripture: 1 Corinthians 1:11

Context: Chloe's household reported divisions and quarrels among the Corinthian believers to Paul. This demonstrates her spiritual attentiveness and care for the unity of the body of Christ. Her example reminds us that noticing and understanding the challenges in our community is a vital first step in serving God effectively.

Application: Pay attention to your church and community. Speak up prayerfully when you see areas needing care.

December 2 – Faithful Communication

Scripture: 1 Corinthians 1:11

Context: Chloe's willingness to relay important information allowed Paul to address problems constructively. Her communication was motivated by love for the church and concern for truth, showing that God uses careful reporting and discernment to guide corrective action in ministry.

Application: Share observations with love and truth to strengthen the body of Christ.

December 3 – Influence Through Integrity

Scripture: Proverbs 11:3

Context: Chloe's integrity made her report trustworthy. Leaders and fellow believers are more likely to listen and act when those providing insight have proven faithfulness and honesty. God honours those who lead with integrity, even in seemingly small acts.

Application: Build trust through your actions today; let integrity guide your influence.

December 4 – Praying for Wisdom

Scripture: James 1:5

Context: Chloe's discernment likely came through prayer. God equips those who ask for wisdom to handle complex situations. Spiritual insight combined with prayer enables believers to make godly decisions that influence their community positively.

Application: Ask God for wisdom to act faithfully in church or family matters.

December 5 – Courage in Observation

Scripture: 1 Corinthians 1:11

Context: It takes courage to notice and act on issues others may ignore. Chloe's example shows that careful observation, coupled with prayer and discretion, is a form of godly leadership that can prevent greater harm and foster unity.

Application: Pray for courage to see and act on what God is showing you.

December 6 – Active Partnership

Scripture: Ecclesiastes 4:9–10

Context: Chloe's faithfulness supported Paul's ministry indirectly but powerfully. God often calls believers to partner behind the scenes, through prayer, counsel, and material or logistical support, showing that influence does not always require the spotlight.

Application: Support leaders in your sphere through prayer, counsel, or practical help.

December 7 – Accountability Matters

Scripture: Proverbs 27:17

Context: Chloe's household allowed the Corinthian church to be accountable. Accountability strengthens communities and encourages spiritual growth. Chloe's faithful reporting demonstrates that caring for others sometimes means helping them face uncomfortable truths in love.

Application: Be accountable and support accountability in others for growth in faith.

Week 2

Joanna: Sacrificial Support & Ministry

Scripture: Luke 8:1–3

Scripture: 2 Corinthians 9:7

Context: Joanna's giving was intentional and joyful, not out of obligation. God values a cheerful, willing heart. Her generosity demonstrates that sacrificial giving contributes directly to ministry impact.

December 11 – Influence Through Support

Scripture: Philippians 1:3–5

Context: Joanna's active support strengthened Jesus' ministry and encouraged other disciples. Supportive acts, though often unseen, have a multiplying effect on Kingdom work.

Application: Consider how your actions or resources can encourage or enable others in ministry.

Scripture: Matthew 5:16

Context: Joanna's generosity reflected God's love, serving as a testimony to those around her. Generosity is both practical help and a visible witness that draws others to Christ.

Application: Let your acts of giving and support point others to God.

December 13 – Persistent Faithfulness

Scripture: Galatians 6:9

Context: Joanna's consistent support, though unseen at times, contributed to long-term results. God honours those who persevere in service and faithfulness, showing that persistence matters as much as action.

Application: Keep faithfully serving, even when it feels unnoticed.

December 14 – Partnership in Prayer

Scripture: Colossians 4:2

Context: Prayer undergirded Joanna's ministry. Support through prayer strengthens those on the frontlines and allows God's power to work effectively through others.

Application: Partner in prayer for leaders and ministries you support.
