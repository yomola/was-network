July 2026 – Case Study: Mary (Mother of Jesus)

Surrender, Purity of Heart & Obedience

Luke 1:26–56 Luke 2:1–20 John 19:25–27

This month emphasises learning to surrender to God's will with humility, embrace purity of heart in every decision, and obey God even when His plans stretch you beyond your comfort.

Week 1 – Called and Chosen

Week 1

Called and Chosen

Key Scriptures: Luke 1:26–33; Isaiah 7:14

Weekly Focus: Understanding Mary's calling and learning how God calls ordinary people into extraordinary assignments.

July 1 – God Chooses the Humble

Scripture: Luke 1:26–28

Context: Mary lived an ordinary life in a small, overlooked town, yet God sent an angel directly to her. This shows that God sees beyond status and location, He sees the heart. Mary's humility positioned her for divine calling, reminding us that God often works through those the world least expects.

Application: Ask God to cultivate humility in your heart so you can be ready for His assignments.

July 2 – Favoured but Afraid

Scripture: Luke 1:29

Context: Mary was greatly troubled, showing that even the favoured can feel fear when God calls. Divine assignments often stretch us emotionally and spiritually. Mary's initial hesitation reveals her humanity, yet she remained open to God's voice.

Application: Bring your fears before God and invite Him to speak peace into them.

July 3 – God's Presence in Divine Callings

Scripture: Luke 1:30

Context: The angel reassures Mary not to fear because God is with her. Every calling from God is accompanied by His presence, provision, and protection. Mary's story teaches us that we never carry God's assignments alone.

Application: Thank God for His presence in moments that feel overwhelming.

July 4 – God's Plan Exceeds Understanding

Scripture: Luke 1:31–33

Context: The angel reveals that Mary will carry the Messiah, a plan far beyond what she could comprehend. Sometimes God entrusts us with assignments we cannot fully understand at the moment. Faith accepts even what the mind cannot explain.

Application: Surrender something today that you don't fully understand yet.

July 5 – Grace for the Assignment

Scripture: Luke 1:34

Context: Mary questions, "How can this be?" showing that honest questions are part of the journey of faith. God doesn't rebuke her questioning; He responds with clarity. Grace allows room for curiosity without condemnation.

Application: Ask God sincere questions about your calling; trust Him to guide you.

July 6 – The Power of the Holy Spirit

Scripture: Luke 1:35

Context: The angel reveals that the Holy Spirit will overshadow Mary. What God calls you to do, His Spirit empowers you to accomplish. Mary was not to rely on her strength but on divine empowerment.

Application: Invite the Holy Spirit to empower your weaknesses today.

July 7 – Nothing Is Impossible With God

Scripture: Luke 1:37

Context: Mary receives the truth that God can do what seems impossible. This verse anchors her calling and offers strength for the unknown. It reminds us that God's promises stand above natural limitations.

Application: Write down one "impossible" situation and declare God's truth over it.

Week 2 – Surrendered Obedience

Week 2

Surrendered Obedience

Key Scriptures: Luke 1:38–45

Weekly Focus: Learning to embrace God's will even when it disrupts expectations and comfort.

July 8 – Let It Be to Me

Scripture: Luke 1:38

Context: Mary's surrender is immediate and wholehearted. She chooses obedience without seeing how everything will unfold. This reveals a heart deeply anchored in trust and submission.

Application: Surrender one area of resistance to God today.

July 9 – Trusting God's Word

Scripture: Luke 1:39–40

Context: Mary travels to Elizabeth, already acting in faith. Her obedience shows trust in God's word even before seeing its fulfilment.

Application: Take one step today toward something God has spoken to you.

July 10 – The Blessing of Godly Community

Scripture: Luke 1:41

Context: Elizabeth's baby leaps at Mary's greeting, signalling divine confirmation. God often uses community to affirm His work in us.

Application: Seek or appreciate the godly voices who confirm God's direction in your life.

July 11 – Confirmation of Calling

Scripture: Luke 1:42

Context: Elizabeth blesses Mary, affirming her role in God's plan. Encouragement strengthens obedience, especially during uncertain seasons.

Application: Encourage someone's calling today.

July 12 – Blessed for Believing

Scripture: Luke 1:45

Context: Elizabeth celebrates Mary's faith, reminding us that blessing is connected to trusting God's word. Mary's belief becomes her strength.

Application: Reflect on areas God is calling you to deeper belief.

July 13 – Joy in God's Will

Scripture: Luke 1:46–47

Context: Mary's soul magnifies the Lord. Joy flows when we trust God's purposes, even when challenges accompany them.

Application: Choose joy today in obedience, not circumstances.

July 14 – Seeing God's Faithfulness

Scripture: Luke 1:48–49

Context: Mary acknowledges God's faithfulness to her lowly estate. God elevates the humble and honours those who yield to Him.

Application: Recall moments when God has been faithful to you.

Week 3 – Embracing God's Purpose Through Life's Weight

Week 3

Embracing God's Purpose Through Life's Weight

Key Scriptures: Luke 2:1–20; Micah 5:2; Isaiah 9:6

Weekly Focus: Understanding how God's purpose unfolds even through discomfort, disruption, and unexpected circumstances.

July 15 – Purpose in the Journey

Scripture: Luke 2:1–3

Context: A decree forces Mary and Joseph to travel to Bethlehem at the worst possible time. Mary is heavily pregnant, far from home, and facing uncertainty. Yet God uses a political order to fulfil prophecy. Even external pressures serve divine purpose.

Application: Trust that God can work through circumstances you did not choose.

July 16 – Obedience in Discomfort

Scripture: Luke 2:4–5

Context: Mary and Joseph obey civil authority even when it disrupts their comfort and security. Their obedience positions them exactly where God wants them to be. Sometimes uncomfortable obedience leads to divine alignment.

Application: Obey God even when the path is inconvenient.

July 17 – No Room but God Still Moves

Scripture: Luke 2:6–7

Context: There was no room for them in the inn. Mary gives birth in a place unfit for a child, yet this humble setting becomes the birthplace of salvation. God's greatest works often start in imperfect conditions.

Application: Invite God into the "manger places" of your life: your least ideal spaces.

July 18 – Treasure in Humility

Scripture: Luke 2:7

Context: Mary wraps Jesus in simple clothes. Though she carries the King of kings, she embraces a humble beginning. She teaches us that purpose isn't diminished by humble surroundings; rather, humility amplifies God's glory.

Application: Embrace humility in how you serve and grow.

July 19 – God Reveals to the Lowly

Scripture: Luke 2:8–10

Context: The first announcement of Jesus' birth isn't made to royalty but to shepherds; people marginalised and overlooked. Mary witnesses that God values the humble, and those hungry to hear Him.

Application: Ask God for the heart of a shepherd; attentive, humble, and open.

July 20 – Awe in God's Plan

Scripture: Luke 2:11–12

Context: The angel announces a Saviour wrapped in simple clothes. Mary learns that divine purpose often comes clothed in simplicity. God's glory is often hidden in ordinary things.

Application: Look for God in simple and ordinary moments today.

July 21 – Pondering God's Movements

Scripture: Luke 2:19

Context: Mary treasures and ponders everything. Instead of rushing or reacting, she reflects deeply. This practice of spiritual reflection shapes her faith and sustains her through later trials.

Application: Pause today to meditate on something God has done recently.

Week 4 – Faithful Through Pain and Maturity

Week 4

Faithful Through Pain and Maturity

Key Scriptures: Luke 2:25–35; John 19:25–27

Weekly Focus: Learning steadfastness, emotional resilience, and faith through seasons of joy, uncertainty, and great pain.

July 22 – Hearing God Through Others

Scripture: Luke 2:25–27

Context: Simeon, led by the Spirit, speaks over Mary and Jesus. Mary learns that God uses prophetic voices to affirm and clarify our journey. God guides His children through spiritual community.

Application: Listen for God's voice through trusted spiritual people.

July 23 – A Sword Will Pierce Your Soul

Scripture: Luke 2:34–35

Context: Simeon reveals that Mary's journey will involve deep emotional pain. Following God doesn't shield us from sorrow; sometimes obedience increases it. Yet God prepares her with truth, not illusions.

Application: Ask God for strength to remain faithful in painful seasons.

July 24 – Growing in Wisdom as a Parent

Scripture: Luke 2:40

Context: Jesus grows strong in spirit, and Mary witnesses the unfolding of God's purpose little by little. Growth is a process, and God invites us to participate patiently.

Application: Be patient with what God is growing in your life.

July 25 – When You Don't Understand

Scripture: Luke 2:48–50

Context: Mary loses Jesus for three days, and when she finds Him, she doesn't fully understand His explanation. Even the most faithful believers experience confusion. God often works beyond our understanding.

Application: Release the need to understand everything God is doing.

July 26 – Keeping God's Words in Your Heart

Scripture: Luke 2:51

Context: Mary continues to treasure God's words. Her spiritual discipline of reflection helps her navigate the complexities of raising the Messiah. Intimacy with God strengthens obedience.

Application: Spend intentional time in God's presence today.

July 27 – Standing at the Cross

Scripture: John 19:25

Context: Mary stands near the cross as her Son suffers. Her endurance shows that true devotion endures even through unbearable pain. She models unwavering faithfulness.

Application: Ask God to help you remain faithful in the hardest places.

July 28 – Receiving Care from Jesus

Scripture: John 19:26–27

Context: Even in agony, Jesus provides for Mary, entrusting her to John. Mary learns that God cares for her personal needs even while fulfilling cosmic plans.

Application: Trust God to care for personal burdens you carry.

July 29 – Faith After Loss

Scripture: Acts 1:14

Context: Mary is found with the disciples in prayer after Jesus' ascension. Despite unimaginable loss, she continues to believe, worship, and wait. She embodies enduring faith.

Application: Continue trusting God in areas of life where you've experienced loss.

July 30 – Legacy of Obedience

Scripture: Luke 1:50

Context: Mary's obedience becomes a testimony from generation to generation. Her "yes" to God shapes the world. Kingdom legacy begins with one surrendered decision.

Application: Consider what legacy your obedience is shaping.

July 31 – Becoming a Vessel of God's Will

Scripture: Luke 1:38

Context: Mary ends where she began; surrendering to God's plan. Her life shows that God uses ordinary people who willingly yield to Him. True greatness is found in surrender.

Application: Pray a prayer of complete surrender for the month ahead.
