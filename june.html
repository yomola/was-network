June 2026 – Case Study: Shiphrah & Puah

Courage, Justice & Fear of God

Exodus 1:8–21

This month emphasises responding with courage and integrity in the face of oppression, obeying God above human authority, and protecting life even under threat. The midwives risked their lives to save the Hebrew babies because they feared God, demonstrating bold obedience, justice, and faith-driven action.

Week 1 – Encountering Their Story

Week 1

Encountering Their Story

Key Scriptures: Exodus 1:8–14; Proverbs 31:8–9; Psalm 82:3–4

Weekly Focus: Seeing oppression clearly and responding with courage and conviction.

June 1 – Courage in a Time of Oppression

Scripture: Exodus 1:8–10

Context: A new Pharaoh arises who does not know Joseph or Israel's history. His insecurity and fear lead to policies of oppression. Shiphrah and Puah live and serve in this hostile climate, where their faith is tested daily. Their courage begins long before they confront Pharaoh, it begins by choosing faithfulness in an oppressive environment.

Application: Ask God to show you how to respond courageously to the challenges and pressures around you.

June 2 – Seeing the Heart of the People

Scripture: Psalm 82:3–4

Context: God commands His people to defend the weak, uphold justice, and rescue the oppressed. Shiphrah and Puah embody this command as they stand between life and death for vulnerable infants. Their awareness of suffering leads them to righteous action.

Application: Pray for sensitivity to injustice around you and courage to respond with compassion.

June 3 – Obedience Over Fear

Scripture: Exodus 1:11–12

Context: Despite Pharaoh's harsh labour policies, God strengthens His people. The Israelites multiply even under pressure; showing that divine favour can flourish in difficult seasons. Shiphrah and Puah knew God's presence was with Israel, which emboldened them to obey Him rather than fear Pharaoh.

Application: Trust God to protect and guide you when obeying Him feels risky.

June 4 – Integrity in Your Role

Scripture: Proverbs 31:8–9

Context: God calls His people to speak for the voiceless. Shiphrah and Puah had a unique position, midwives with power to protect life. Integrity in their role became a matter of life and death, reminding us that our positions are opportunities for justice.

Application: Identify an area in your life or work where integrity requires bold action.

June 5 – Choosing Courage Daily

Scripture: Exodus 1:13–14

Context: Israel's oppression intensifies as Pharaoh pushes them into bitter and exhausting labour. In such an environment, daily courage becomes essential—not occasional heroism but persistent faithfulness.

Application: Commit to one courageous action today, no matter how small it seems.

June 6 – God Works Through the Faithful

Scripture: Exodus 1:15–16

Context: Pharaoh directly commands the midwives to kill Hebrew baby boys. This is a moment of moral crisis, where obedience to God will put them in direct conflict with national power. Their fear of God anchors their decision.

Application: Pray for strength to obey God above all, even when the cost feels high.

June 7 – Fear God Above All

Scripture: Acts 5:29

Context: Peter and the apostles echo the same conviction Shiphrah and Puah lived: God's command outweighs human command. Obedience to God becomes the ultimate priority in every era.

Application: Reflect on an area where God's commands may conflict with the expectations of others.

Week 2 – The Midwives' Fear of God

Week 2

The Midwives' Fear of God

Key Scriptures: Exodus 1:15–17; Proverbs 29:25; Acts 5:29

Weekly Focus: Choosing to obey God above human authority, social pressure, or personal fear.

June 8 – Fear God, Not Man

Scripture: Exodus 1:17

Context: Shiphrah and Puah refuse Pharaoh's order because they fear God. This "fear" is reverence, loyalty, and deep spiritual conviction. Their moral compass isn't tied to the king's wrath but to God's standards of justice.

Application: Where do you need to choose God's approval over human approval?

June 9 – Courage Requires Wisdom

Scripture: Proverbs 11:14

Context: The midwives acted courageously and wisely. Their refusal to obey came with strategic thinking, not reckless impulse. Godly courage is often strengthened by wise counsel and discernment.

Application: Seek godly counsel before making a bold decision.

June 10 – Obedience Can Be Dangerous

Scripture: Acts 5:29

Context: Obeying God in a hostile environment has consequences. Shiphrah and Puah risked their careers, safety, and possibly their lives. Scripture reminds us that obedience to God often invites opposition but God honours it.

Application: Pray for courage to obey God even when the cost feels heavy.

June 11 – Integrity Under Pressure

Scripture: Proverbs 10:9

Context: Walking in integrity provides spiritual security. The midwives stood firm under intense pressure, proving that integrity is often tested most in difficult seasons.

Application: Reflect on one area where you need to practice integrity today.

June 12 – Hidden Heroes

Scripture: Hebrews 11:35

Context: God's hall of faith includes many unnamed heroes. Shiphrah and Puah demonstrate how ordinary people in humble roles can shape history through faith. Faithfulness in quiet places is powerful.

Application: Be faithful in small, unseen tasks today.

June 13 – Courage Inspires Others

Scripture: 1 Timothy 4:12

Context: No one is too insignificant or powerless to inspire courage. Shiphrah and Puah's example influenced Israel and generations to come. Your courage also becomes a testimony.

Application: Identify someone you can inspire by your example today.

June 14 – God's Presence Is Your Shield

Scripture: Psalm 91:4

Context: God covers and protects His faithful servants. The midwives were shielded from Pharaoh's wrath not by chance but by divine intervention.

Application: Ask God to shield and strengthen you today.

Context: God celebrates faithfulness in "little things." The midwives' simple act of protecting newborns altered Israel's entire future.
