November 2026 – Case Study: Lydia

Hospitality, Evangelism & Kingdom Influence

Acts 16:11–40 Philippians 1:3–6 Matthew 5:14–16

This month emphasises using your home, gifts, and influence to serve God's Kingdom. Lydia demonstrates a heart open to God, practical hospitality, and active participation in spreading the gospel.

Week 1 – Lydia's Encounter with Paul

Week 1

Lydia's Encounter with Paul

Key Scriptures: Acts 16:11–15; John 6:44; Romans 10:17

Weekly Focus: A heart opened by God, ready to respond to His call.

November 1 – Divine Appointment

Scripture: Acts 16:11–13

Context: Paul and his companions arrive in Philippi and go to the riverside where people gather for prayer. Lydia, a woman engaged in business and known for her devotion, is already positioned where God intends to meet her. What looks like an ordinary gathering becomes the moment heaven intersects with her life.

Application: Be attentive to God's voice today. Ordinary spaces often become the place where God redirects your story.

November 2 – Openness of Heart

Scripture: Acts 16:14

Context: Lydia listens to Paul's message, but her transformation begins when God Himself opens her heart. This shows that salvation is always a divine work. He prepares the heart long before the moment of decision. Lydia's openness reveals humility, hunger, and readiness for truth.

Application: Ask God to give you a soft, teachable heart. Stay open to His promptings throughout the day.

November 3 – Response in Faith

Scripture: Acts 16:15

Context: Lydia responds immediately, choosing baptism as a public declaration of her new faith. She does not delay obedience or keep her transformation private, her whole household witnesses and follows her lead. Her quick response demonstrates sincerity and spiritual clarity.

Application: When God speaks, respond promptly. Delayed obedience weakens faith; quick obedience strengthens it.

November 4 – Faith That Acts

Scripture: Romans 10:17

Context: Lydia's faith began with hearing the word of God. The message didn't just inspire her; it moved her into action. She embodies the truth that faith is not passive; it grows when we hear, believe, and take steps in alignment with God's voice.

Application: Let today's faith move you toward a specific step of obedience or spiritual growth.

November 5 – A Life That Draws Others

Scripture: John 6:44

Context: Lydia's conversion is not an isolated event. As God draws her, her life becomes a witness that draws others; her family, her community, and the early church in Philippi. Her openness becomes a doorway through which others encounter God.

Application: Live in a way that gently draws others toward Christ. Your faith can influence far more than you realise.

November 6 – Preparing the Way

Scripture: Acts 16:15

Context: Lydia immediately opens her home to Paul and his companions, showing that her faith is not theoretical; it's practical. Her hospitality becomes the very setting where ministry, teaching, and discipleship take root in Philippi. God often uses prepared hearts to create prepared spaces.

Application: Offer your gifts, time, and resources to make room for God's work.

November 7 – Faith That Creates Space

Scripture: Acts 16:15

Context: Lydia's home becomes a safe place for the apostles and, eventually, the entire church. Her willingness to create space becomes a key factor in establishing the gospel in a new region. Sometimes influence begins simply by opening a door.

Application: Use your home, talents, or time to create room for others to grow spiritually.

Week 2 – Hospitality as Ministry

Week 2

Hospitality as Ministry

Key Scriptures: Acts 16:15; Hebrews 13:2; 1 Peter 4:8–10

Weekly Focus: Making your home and life a place of service and ministry.

November 8 – Welcoming Others

Scripture: Hebrews 13:2

Context: Scripture reminds believers to show hospitality because we may be serving angels without knowing it. Lydia's generosity reveals a heart that welcomes God by welcoming others. Hospitality becomes a spiritual discipline, not merely an act of kindness.

Application: Practice welcoming others with warmth and grace today.

November 9 – Generosity in Action

Scripture: 1 Peter 4:9–10

Context: God calls His people to serve without complaining and to use their gifts faithfully. Lydia's generosity reflects this truth; her open home becomes a centre of ministry. She gives joyfully, turning her resources into tools for kingdom work.

Application: Serve others wholeheartedly today. Let generosity flow freely.

November 10 – Faithful Stewardship

Scripture: Acts 16:15

Context: Lydia demonstrates that stewardship isn't only about money; it's about using one's life, influence, and environment for God's glory. Her faith turns her household into a platform for spiritual transformation.

Application: Offer what you have to God and ask Him to use it for His purposes.

November 11 – Kingdom Influence

Scripture: Matthew 5:14–16

Context: Lydia shines brightly in her city through hospitality and faith. She becomes a living example of Jesus' call to let your light shine before others. Her daily life becomes a witness that extends beyond words.

Application: Let your life today reflect Christ's light in practical, visible ways.

November 12 – Open Hands, Open Heart

Scripture: Acts 16:15

Context: Lydia's heart posture is mirrored in her open hands. Her willingness to give, host, and serve shows that generosity flows out of a surrendered spirit. God uses open-hearted believers to build His Kingdom.

Application: Be open today; open-handed, open-hearted, and open to God's assignments.

November 13 – Hospitality Honours God

Scripture: 1 Peter 4:10

Context: Every believer carries a grace-gift designed to serve others. Lydia honours God not through preaching but through consistent service. This reminds us that ministry happens in quiet corners as much as public platforms.

Application: Serve with intentional love today, knowing God sees every act.

November 14 – Faith Multiplies Influence

Scripture: Acts 16:15

Context: Lydia's faith and hospitality transform her home into a ministry base. Her willingness to stand with the apostles becomes a catalyst for the spread of the gospel in Philippi. Influence grows where faithfulness is present.

Application: Allow your obedience to become a seed of influence in the lives around you.

Week 3 – Business and Influence

Week 3

Business and Influence

Key Scriptures: Proverbs 31:13–24; Colossians 3:23; Deuteronomy 8:18

Weekly Focus: Using professional gifts and skills to advance God's kingdom.

November 15 – Diligent Work

Scripture: Proverbs 31:13

Context: Lydia's trade in purple cloth required skill, craftsmanship, and reputation. Her success shows diligence is not only practical but spiritual; God honours excellence.

Application: Work with excellence today, knowing your labour honours God.

November 16 – Excellence in Trade

Scripture: Proverbs 31:14

Context: Like the virtuous woman, Lydia's work extended beyond herself. Her business brought value to others and opened doors for ministry. She demonstrates how faith and professionalism complement each other.

Application: Offer quality and integrity in all your work interactions today.

November 17 – Godly Stewardship

Scripture: Deuteronomy 8:18

Context: Scripture reminds us that God is the source of ability, strength, and prosperity. Lydia recognises this truth in how she uses her business to support ministry.

Application: Acknowledge God as the giver of your gifts and use them wisely.

November 18 – Faithful Management

Scripture: Proverbs 31:16

Context: The wise woman invests with foresight and discipline. Lydia likewise manages her resources with purpose, showing that godly influence requires intentional stewardship.

Application: Manage what God has entrusted to you with wisdom and prayer.

November 19 – Kingdom-Oriented Work

Scripture: Colossians 3:23

Context: Lydia's work ethic reflects serving the Lord, not just clients. Her business becomes a channel of influence because her heart is anchored in God's kingdom.

Application: Work today with the mindset that God is your true employer.

November 20 – Prosperity for Purpose

Scripture: Proverbs 31:18

Context: Lydia's prosperity doesn't serve vanity; it serves ministry. Her success becomes an avenue to support and shelter God's servants. Prosperity becomes meaningful when aligned with purpose.

Application: Use success as a platform to bless others and honour God.

November 21 – Opportunity Meets Preparation

Scripture: Proverbs 31:20

Context: Lydia's generosity comes from preparation; her diligence enables her to give freely. When God brought opportunity, she was ready.

Application: Prepare your heart and resources so you can respond when God opens a door.

Week 4 – The First Church in Philippi

Week 4

The First Church in Philippi

Key Scriptures: Acts 16:40; Philippians 1:3–6; Matthew 5:14–16

Weekly Focus: Building spiritual community and sustaining ministry.

November 22 – Opening Her Home

Scripture: Acts 16:40

Context: After Paul and Silas are released from prison, they return to Lydia's home; a place of spiritual refuge and encouragement. Her household becomes the first church in Philippi, demonstrating how God uses open homes to build His kingdom.

Application: Open your home or life to foster fellowship and support.

November 23 – Gratitude in Leadership

Scripture: Philippians 1:3–4

Context: Paul consistently remembers the believers of Philippi with joy. Lydia's partnership in ministry is foundational to this gratitude. Her support illustrates how every role, visible or not, strengthens the church.

Application: Give thanks for those who serve with you. Encourage someone today.

November 24 – God Completes His Work

Scripture: Philippians 1:6

Context: Paul assures Lydia's community that God will finish what He starts. Lydia's obedience is evidence, what began at a riverside grew into a thriving church. God's work never ends at the moment of salvation; it continues through our lives.

Application: Trust God to finish what He starts in you. Surrender today's steps to Him.

November 25 – Shine Your Light

Scripture: Matthew 5:14–16

Context: Lydia's visible faith influences her city. She becomes a lamp in Philippi; shining through service, generosity, and courage. Her life proves that influence doesn't require a title, just obedience.

Application: Let your faith shine naturally today through love and kindness.

November 26 – Sustaining Community

Scripture: Acts 16:40

Context: Lydia continues supporting the believers even after Paul departs. Her home becomes an anchor for new believers developing in faith. Sustaining community requires consistency, warmth, and spiritual commitment.

Application: Support your spiritual community with your presence and gifts.

November 27 – Faithfulness in Action

Scripture: Philippians 1:3–6

Context: Paul's words reflect the ongoing faithfulness of believers like Lydia. Her steady support becomes the foundation for a strong church. Everyday obedience creates long-term fruit.

Application: Remain faithful in the assignments God has given you.

November 28 – Influence Through Service

Scripture: Matthew 5:16

Context: Lydia doesn't preach sermons, yet her service preaches loudly. Her acts of hospitality and kindness illuminate the gospel to her community. Influence grows wherever service is present.

Application: Serve someone today in a way that reveals God's love.

November 29 – Building Spiritual Legacy

Scripture: Acts 16:15

Context: Lydia's obedience becomes the seed of a spiritual legacy that outlives her. Her home becomes a church; her generosity becomes the foundation of a movement. Faith lived out fully always leaves a mark on eternity.

Application: Pour into people and places that build lasting spiritual legacy.

November 30 – Faithful Stewardship Complete

Scripture: Philippians 1:6

Context: Lydia's story ends as a reminder that the God who begins good work continues and completes it. Her life demonstrates that God uses ordinary obedience to accomplish extraordinary kingdom impact.

Application: Continue serving faithfully. Trust God to complete His work through you.
