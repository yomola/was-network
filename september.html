September 2026 – Case Study: Jochebed

Faith, Protection & Sacrificial Love

Exodus 2:1–10 Hebrews 11:23 Exodus 3:1–12

Jochebed exemplifies unwavering faith and sacrificial love, trusting God to protect her child even in the face of danger.

Week 1 – Faith in the Midst of Danger

Week 1

Faith in the Midst of Danger

Key Scriptures: Exodus 1:22–2:10; Hebrews 11:23; Psalm 91:1–4

Weekly Focus: Acting in faith to preserve life even when circumstances are oppressive.

September 1 – Courage in a Dangerous Time

Scripture: Exodus 1:22–2:2

Context: Pharaoh orders the killing of all Hebrew male infants. Jochebed faces a terrifying reality: the survival of her child is at risk. Her courage emerges not in comfort but under extreme threat. She models faith that acts decisively when life itself is in danger.

Application: Ask God to give you courage to act righteously, even when situations feel overwhelming. How can faith guide your actions in the face of fear?

September 2 – Sacrificial Love in Action

Scripture: Exodus 2:1–2

Context: Jochebed's love for Moses is willing to endure risk and personal pain. True love often requires sacrifice and trusting God with outcomes beyond our control.

Application: Identify a situation where sacrificial love could honour God today. Where is God calling you to trust Him with someone you love?

September 3 – Creative Faith

Scripture: Exodus 2:3

Context: She places Moses in a basket and sets him among the reeds. Jochebed demonstrates that faith sometimes requires creativity and careful planning, even under pressure. God uses practical, thoughtful action alongside trust in Him.

Application: Pray for wisdom to act faithfully in your current challenges. What practical steps can you take today to obey God faithfully?

September 4 – Trusting God's Protection

Scripture: Psalm 91:1–4

Context: Despite the danger, Jochebed trusts that God will protect her child. Faith is the acknowledgement that God's protection is greater than human threats.

Application: Ask God for protection over your family and responsibilities, trusting His sovereignty. How does trusting God change your approach to fear?

September 5 – Bold Obedience in Small Acts

Scripture: Hebrews 11:23

Context: By hiding Moses for three months, Jochebed participates in God's plan in a seemingly small but monumental act of faith. Small acts of obedience can carry eternal significance.

Application: Identify one unseen but faithful act you can do today. What small action today could have lasting impact?

September 6 – Letting Go With Confidence

Scripture: Exodus 2:4–5

Context: Jochebed places Moses in Pharaoh's daughter's care, letting go of control. Faith sometimes means releasing what we treasure most into God's hands.

Application: Surrender a personal concern to God with confidence in His care. What do you need to release to God today?

September 7 – Faithful Waiting

Scripture: Exodus 2:6–7

Context: Even after placing Moses in the basket, Jochebed continues her care and watches over him. Faithful waiting is active, patient, and trusting.

Application: Ask God for patience and strength to wait faithfully in uncertain circumstances. How can you practice patient trust this week?

Obedience and Providential Care

Key Scriptures: Exodus 2:1–10; Exodus 3:1–12; Proverbs 3:5–6

Weekly Focus: Acting faithfully while trusting God's timing and guidance.

September 8 – God's Timing is Perfect

Scripture: Exodus 2:1–2

Context: Moses' birth and protection occur at precisely the right moment. Jochebed's obedience aligns perfectly with God's timing, demonstrating that faith requires both action and patience.

Application: Pray for discernment to act according to God's timing. Where is God asking you to wait faithfully?

September 9 – Obedience Despite Risk

Scripture: Exodus 2:3–4

Context: Placing Moses in the Nile was dangerous, yet Jochebed obeys. Faith often requires risking comfort, security, and even personal safety for God's purposes.

Application: Identify one area where God calls you to bold obedience. What risks are you willing to take for faithfulness?

September 10 – Trusting God with Others

Scripture: Exodus 2:5–6

Context: God orchestrates Pharaoh's daughter to find Moses. Jochebed trusts God to work through others to accomplish His plans. Faith often extends beyond what we can directly control.

Application: Surrender someone or something you care about into God's hands today. How can you trust God to work through people around you?

September 11 – God's Providence in Small Details

Scripture: Exodus 2:7–10

Context: Every detail from Pharaoh's daughter noticing Moses to Jochebed being able to nurse him, reveals God's providential care. Obedience meets God's power in even the smallest actions.

Application: Look for evidence of God's guidance in the details of your life. How has God been faithful in the "small" things recently?

September 12 – Courageous Love Leaves a Legacy

Scripture: Hebrews 11:23

Context: Jochebed's courageous act shapes the future of Israel. Acts of sacrificial love create enduring legacies.

Application: Identify one way your love and courage can impact the next generation. What legacy are you building through your faith today?

September 13 – Protecting Life Through Faith

Scripture: John 10:10; Psalm 127:3

Context: Jochebed's protective actions demonstrate that valuing life and trusting God to preserve it honours His creation. Faith and love work together to defend what is precious.

Application: Commit to protecting life; spiritual, emotional, or physical, in your sphere today. Who or what needs your protection this week?

September 14 – Waiting with Hope

Scripture: Psalm 37:3–6

Context: Jochebed waited in hope and faith, trusting God for Moses' future. Hope sustains faith in long seasons of uncertainty.

Application: Pray for renewed hope in areas where waiting feels endless. How does hope strengthen your faith in challenging situations?
