      <div class="days-grid">
        <article class="day">
          <div class="day-header">
            <span class="day-date">May 1</span>
            <span class="day-title">– Faithful in Hardship</span>
          </div>
          <div class="day-body">
            <p><strong>Scripture:</strong> Ruth 1:1–2</p>
            <p>
              <strong>Context:</strong> Ruth's story begins in a time of famine and national hardship. Naomi loses her husband and sons, illustrating that life's difficulties can test relationships and faith. Ruth, as a Moabite widow, faces uncertainty, cultural displacement, and social vulnerability. Her choice to remain loyal reflects a heart aligned with God's purposes.
            </p>
            <p>
              <strong>Application:</strong> Reflect on someone you can support faithfully, even when situations are difficult. Consider the practical ways you can demonstrate loyalty.
            </p>
          </div>
        </article>

        <article class="day">
          <div class="day-header">
            <span class="day-date">May 2</span>
            <span class="day-title">– Choosing Loyalty</span>
          </div>
          <div class="day-body">
            <p><strong>Scripture:</strong> Ruth 1:3–5</p>
            <p>
              <strong>Context:</strong> Naomi's personal tragedy could have justified Ruth leaving for safety and security. Yet Ruth chooses to stay, showing that loyalty is an intentional choice, not just a feeling. God honours such committed hearts, which reflect His steadfast love toward us.
            </p>
            <p>
              <strong>Application:</strong> Ask God to help you choose loyalty in relationships and commitments today, even when it's inconvenient.
            </p>
          </div>
        </article>

        <article class="day">
          <div class="day-header">
            <span class="day-date">May 3</span>
            <span class="day-title">– Where You Go, I Will Go</span>
          </div>
          <div class="day-body">
            <p><strong>Scripture:</strong> Ruth 1:6–10</p>
            <p>
              <strong>Context:</strong> Ruth pledges to accompany Naomi despite uncertainty. Leaving her homeland, family, and culture demonstrates a deep trust in both Naomi and God. Loyalty sometimes demands stepping into the unknown, where faith becomes action. God often blesses those who are willing to leave familiarity for obedience and love.
            </p>
            <p>
              <strong>Application:</strong> Write down one decision requiring faith and commitment this week and ask God to give courage.
            </p>
          </div>
        </article>

          <div class="day-header">

          </div>
          <div class="day-body">
            <p><strong>Scripture:</strong> Ruth 1:11–13</p>
            <p>
              <strong>Context:</strong> Naomi attempts to release Ruth from obligation, yet Ruth's heart remains steadfast. Loyalty strengthens relationships and preserves opportunities for God's providence to unfold. God values obedience and enduring love above convenience.
            </p>
            <p>
              <strong>Application:</strong> Reflect on a relationship that needs loyalty and prayerful support.
            </p>
          </div>
        </article>

          </div>
          <div class="day-body">
            <p><strong>Scripture:</strong> Ruth 1:14–15</p>
            <p>
              <strong>Context:</strong> Ruth leaves the familiarity of her homeland for an uncertain future in Bethlehem. Her loyalty is costly and requires leaving cultural security and comfort behind. True faithfulness sometimes requires personal sacrifice, yet God honours such commitment by using it to accomplish His plans.

          </div>

            <p><strong>Scripture:</strong> Ruth 1:16</p>
            <p>
              <strong>Context:</strong> Ruth declares her enduring loyalty to Naomi: "Where you go, I will go;....." This level of commitment reflects covenantal love, mirroring God's own faithfulness to His people. Her choice is a model of faithful obedience and selfless love that sets the stage for blessing.

              <strong>Application:</strong> Journal ways you can show unwavering support in your closest relationships.

            <span class="day-title">– Faithful Choices Matter</span>
          </div>
          <div class="day-body">
            <p><strong>Scripture:</strong> Ruth 1:17–18</p>
            <p>
              <strong>Context:</strong> Ruth's steadfast decisions strengthen Naomi and create a foundation for God's providential plans. The choices we make faithfully, even in small ways, can influence the destiny of others and align with God's greater purposes. Loyalty and commitment often unlock unseen blessings.
            </p>
            <p>
              <strong>Application:</strong> Reflect on one choice today that can bless someone else long-term.
